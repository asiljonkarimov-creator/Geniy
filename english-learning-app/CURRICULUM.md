# 📚 1 YILLIK INGLIZ TILI O'QUV REJASI (5 YOSHLI BOLALAR UCHUN)

## 🎯 Umumiy Maqsad
5 yoshli bolani ingliz tili bilan tanishtirib, asosiy so'zlar, oddiy gaplar va kun kunlik iboralarni o'rgatish.

---

## 📅 OY BO'YICHA TAQSIMLASH

### 🌟 1-OY: ALIFBO VA ASOSIY TOVUSHLAR (Alphabet & Basic Sounds)
**Hafta 1-2:** A-M harflari
- Harflarni tanish, yozish va talaffuz qilish
- Har harf uchun 2-3 ta oddiy so'z (A - Apple, Ant, Alligator)

**Hafta 3-4:** N-Z harflari
- Qolgan harflarni o'rganish
- Oddiy so'zlarni aytish mashqlari

**O'yinlar:** 
- Harflarni topish o'yini
- Tovush va harf moslashtirish

---

### 🎨 2-OY: RANGLAR VA SHAKLLAR (Colors & Shapes)
**Hafta 1:** Asosiy ranglar
- Red, Blue, Yellow, Green, Orange, Purple, Pink, Black, White

**Hafta 2:** Shakllar
- Circle, Square, Triangle, Rectangle, Star, Heart

**Hafta 3-4:** Takrorlash va amaliyot
- Rangli narsalarni topish
- Shakllarni chizish va nom berish

**O'yinlar:**
- Rang topish o'yini
- Shakllarni moslashtirish

---

### 🔢 3-OY: RAQAMLAR 1-20 (Numbers)
**Hafta 1-2:** 1-10 gacha
- Raqamlarni sanash
- Narsalarni sanash (1 apple, 2 balls...)

**Hafta 3-4:** 11-20 gacha
- Davom etgan sanash
- Oddiy matematik amallar (qo'shish)

**O'yinlar:**
- Sanash o'yini
- Narsalarni guruhlashtirish

---

### 🐾 4-OY: HAYVONLAR (Animals)
**Hafta 1:** Uy hayvonlari
- Cat, Dog, Fish, Bird, Rabbit, Hamster

**Hafta 2:** Yovvoyi hayvonlar
- Lion, Tiger, Elephant, Monkey, Giraffe, Bear

**Hafta 3:** Qushlar va hasharotlar
- Parrot, Owl, Butterfly, Bee, Ladybug

**Hafta 4:** Takrorlash va tovushlar
- Hayvon tovushlarini taqlid qilish

**O'yinlar:**
- Hayvon tovushini topish
- Hayvon nomi o'yini

---

### 🍎 5-OY: OVQATLAR (Food & Drinks)
**Hafta 1:** Mevalar
- Apple, Banana, Orange, Grape, Strawberry, Watermelon

**Hafta 2:** Sabzavotlar
- Carrot, Tomato, Potato, Cucumber, Broccoli

**Hafta 3:** Ichimliklar va ovqatlar
- Water, Milk, Juice, Bread, Cheese, Egg

**Hafta 4:** "I like..." gaplari
- I like apples. I like milk.

**O'yinlar:**
- Ovqat topish o'yini
- Sevimli ovqatni tanlash

---

### 👨‍👩‍👧 6-OY: OILA VA TANALAR (Family & Body Parts)
**Hafta 1-2:** Oila a'zolari
- Mother, Father, Sister, Brother, Grandma, Grandpa, Baby

**Hafta 3-4:** Tana a'zolari
- Head, Eyes, Nose, Mouth, Ears, Hands, Legs, Feet

**O'yinlar:**
- Kim u? (Who is this?)
- Tana qismlarini ko'rsatish

---

### 👕 7-OY: KIYIMLAR (Clothes)
**Hafta 1-2:** Kundalik kiyimlar
- Shirt, Pants, Dress, Skirt, Shoes, Socks

**Hafta 3:** Qish va yoz kiyimlari
- Coat, Hat, Scarf, Gloves, Shorts, Sandals

**Hafta 4:** "I wear..." gaplari
- I wear a shirt. I wear shoes.

**O'yinlar:**
- Kiyim tanlash o'yini
- Mavsumga mos kiyim topish

---

### 🏠 8-OY: UY VA XONALAR (Home & Rooms)
**Hafta 1-2:** Xonalar
- Bedroom, Kitchen, Bathroom, Living room, Garden

**Hafta 3-4:** Uy buyumlari
- Table, Chair, Bed, Door, Window, TV, Lamp

**O'yinlar:**
- Xonani topish
- Buyumlarni joylashtirish

---

### 🚗 9-OY: TRANSPORT (Vehicles)
**Hafta 1-2:** Kundalik transport
- Car, Bus, Bike, Train, Plane

**Hafta 3-4:** Boshqa transportlar
- Boat, Helicopter, Truck, Motorcycle, Taxi

**O'yinlar:**
- Transport tovushini topish
- Qayerga boramiz?

---

### 🌤️ 10-OY: OB-HAVO VA TABIAT (Weather & Nature)
**Hafta 1:** Ob-havo
- Sunny, Rainy, Cloudy, Windy, Snowy, Hot, Cold

**Hafta 2:** Tabiat
- Tree, Flower, Grass, Sun, Moon, Star, Cloud

**Hafta 3-4:** "It is..." gaplari
- It is sunny. It is cold.

**O'yinlar:**
- Ob-havo topish
- Tabiat rasmlarini moslashtirish

---

### 🎭 11-OY: HARAKATLAR (Actions & Verbs)
**Hafta 1-2:** Oddiy fe'llar
- Run, Jump, Walk, Sit, Stand, Eat, Drink, Sleep

**Hafta 3-4:** Boshqa harakatlar
- Play, Read, Write, Draw, Sing, Dance

**O'yinlar:**
- Harakatni ko'rsatish
- Simon says o'yini

---

### 🎉 12-OY: TAKRORLASH VA BAHOLASH (Review & Assessment)
**Hafta 1:** 1-6 oy materiallarini takrorlash
**Hafta 2:** 7-11 oy materiallarini takrorlash
**Hafta 3:** Interaktiv testlar va o'yinlar
**Hafta 4:** Sertifikat va yutuq bayrami!

**Yakuniy o'yinlar:**
- Barcha mavzular bo'yicha viktorina
- Katta mukofotlar o'yini

---

## 🎮 HAR OY UCHUN O'YINLAR

### Doimiy o'yinlar (har oyda):
1. **Memory Game** - kartalarni moslashtirish
2. **Quiz Time** - savol-javob
3. **Sing Along** - qo'shiq aytish
4. **Story Time** - qisqa hikoyalar
5. **Practice Cards** - flashcard o'yinlari

---

- 🏆 1 medal - 5 dars tugatish
- 🎖️ 1 trophy - 1 oy tugatish
- 🏅 Sertifikat - 1 yil tugatish

- Haftalik hisobot
- Oylik hisobot
- Yillik natija

---

## 💡 OTA-ONALAR UCHUN MASLAHATLAR

1. **Kunlik 15-20 daqiqa** - ortiq vaqt bolani charchatadi
2. **Takrorlash** - bir xil so'zlarni har xil o'yinlarda ishlatish
3. **Maqtash** - har bir yutuqni nishonlash
4. **O'ynash** - o'rganish o'yin orqali bo'lishi kerak
5. **Sabr-toqat** - har bir bola o'z sur'atida o'rganadi
6. **Kun kunlik hayotda** - o'rgangan so'zlarni kundalik ishlatish

---
